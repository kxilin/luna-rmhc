# Luna – RMHC AI Companion

Luna is an AI companion tablet app for families staying at Ronald McDonald House Charities (RMHC) Westmead. She provides emotional support, entertainment, and house information during the "in-between moments" when staff or parents aren't immediately available.

---

## What the Demo Shows

- **Family & profile selection** — families and individuals pick who they are on wake
- **AI chat** — Luna adapts her tone based on the user's age and whether they're a child or parent
- **Music** — calming playlist UI
- **Games** — game selection screen
- **House Info** — Wi-Fi, meal times, facilities, daily notices
- **Contact Front Desk** — message and simulated call UI

---

## Demo Limitations

- Family data and house info are hardcoded — in production, staff manage this through an admin portal
- Music and games are UI only — no audio or interactive gameplay
- Front desk messages are simulated — not connected to real staff
- Voice input is UI only
- No persistent memory between sessions

---

## Admin Portal (Not Built)

In the full product, staff would have a simple web portal to:
- Add and manage family profiles on check-in
- Update meal schedules, notices, and house info in real time
- View mood check-ins and Luna preferences per child
